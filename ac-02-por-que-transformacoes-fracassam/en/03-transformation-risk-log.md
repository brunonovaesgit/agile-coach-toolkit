## Illustrative example (Fictional Bank, investments)

| # | Imagined cause | Pattern | Likelihood | Impact | Preventive action | Owner | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The monthly committee kept deciding everything | 3 and 4 | H | H | Decision matrix and fast track for small changes | Head of investments | Decisions still going to the committee |
| 2 | Product and risk stayed outside the teams | 2 | M | H | Product and risk validation inside the flow from wave 1 | Product manager | Waiting time at validation |
| 3 | We measured squads created, not outcomes | 1 | M | M | Dashboard with lead time and app sign-up drop-off | Coach and head of investments | Dashboard with no business outcome |
| 4 | Executives stopped coming to reviews | 5 | M | H | One increment review a month on leadership's calendar | Head of investments | Reviews with no leader present |
| 5 | We copied cards and changed everything at once | 6 | L | H | Start with app sign-up only | Coach | Wave 2 starting without a wave 1 review |
