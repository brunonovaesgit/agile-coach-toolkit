# Transformation pre-mortem guide

**Goal:** identify, before the start, the most likely reasons the transformation could fail, and turn them into preventive actions.
**When:** after diagnosis and before designing the new model. Short version (45 min) before each wave.
**Participants:** 8 to 15 people. Sponsor, business and technology leaders, team representatives, control functions, HR and finance when possible.
**Duration:** 90 minutes.
**Materials:** board with columns cause | pattern | priority | preventive action | owner | warning sign; sticky notes; six pattern cards (file 02); risk log (file 03).

## Step by step

| Step | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Opening | 10 min | Restate the goal and expected outcome. Agree: every cause is welcome, no one is held to account for what they say. |
| 2. The scene | 5 min | Read aloud: "It's 12 months from now. The transformation failed. Not a small delay: the results didn't come and people went back to the old way." |
| 3. Silent writing | 10 min | In silence, everyone writes every reason they can imagine. One per note. |
| 4. Round | 20 min | One cause per person, in turn, until ideas run out. The sponsor goes first. Nobody answers or defends. |
| 5. Group by pattern | 15 min | Link each cause to one of the six patterns. Anything that doesn't fit becomes a new group. |
| 6. Prioritize | 10 min | Vote on likelihood and impact. Up to five causes. |
| 7. Preventive actions | 15 min | For each cause: one action, one owner, one warning sign. |
| 8. Close | 5 min | What goes into the plan and when the group revisits the list. |

## Facilitation questions
- What happened in the first few weeks that nobody noticed?
- Which decision did we avoid making?
- What did people keep doing the old way, and why?
- Which number showed it was going wrong, and nobody looked?
- What did we promise customers and fail to deliver?

## Remote version
- Digital board with the same columns; anonymous notes for silent writing.
- Round by voice, with the speaking order visible on the board.
- Dot voting to prioritize.
- Large groups: two 45-minute sessions (causes; prioritization and actions).

## After the session
- Actions go into the transformation backlog, written as hypotheses.
- Warning signs go into the signals dashboard (file 06).
- Review the log monthly in the sponsor cadence.

## Traps
- Turning it into a session about the past: return to the imagined future.
- Leaders answering every cause: remind everyone of the agreement.
- A list with no owners.
- Doing it once and never looking again.
