# Checklist before you start

Check before launching the first wave.

- [ ] The goal is written as a business outcome, with a measure and a baseline.
- [ ] The value stream is mapped end to end, with business and control functions.
- [ ] The before-and-after decision matrix has been agreed with leadership.
- [ ] Rules that contradict the new model have been listed and taken to those who decide.
- [ ] Leadership has its own transformation backlog, with owners.
- [ ] The transition is planned in waves, with criteria and hypercare.
- [ ] The pre-mortem is done and its actions are in the backlog.
- [ ] The signals dashboard is agreed, with a baseline.
- [ ] The sponsor cadence is on the calendar.
