# Sponsor interview script

Duration: 60 minutes. Goal: agree on the work before starting.

## 1. Motivation (10 min)
- What made you look for a coach now?
- What has already been tried?
- What happens if nothing changes?

## 2. Outcome (20 min)
- Six months from now, what will be different?
- How will we know? Which number, behavior, or decision will show it?

## 3. Boundaries (20 min)
- What cannot change?
- Who decides what?
- Which areas need to be involved from the start?
- How much of your time can we count on?

## 4. Closing (10 min)
- How will we know the coach's work is done?
- Who carries on afterwards?

## After the conversation
Turn the answers into the coaching agreement canvas and validate it with the sponsor within a week.
