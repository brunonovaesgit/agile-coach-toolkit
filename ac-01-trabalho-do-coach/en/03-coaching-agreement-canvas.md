## Example (illustrative scenario: Fictional Bank)
| Field | Cards at Fictional Bank |
| --- | --- |
| Mission type | Transformation |
| Client | Head of Cards (sponsor) and the cards tribe leads |
| Current situation | Organized by project; every delivery goes through five areas |
| Goal | Reduce the time from approved idea to customer |
| How we will measure | End-to-end time, reviewed monthly by the leadership team |
| In scope | Value stream design, forums, agile roles, rollout in waves |
| Out of scope | Job and salary structure, budget decisions |
| Roles | Sponsor decides; tribe leads co-design; coach facilitates and proposes options |
| Cadence | Monthly review with the sponsor; weekly check-in with the leads |
| Exit criterion | Model running in the cards tribes, with leads running the forums without the coach |
