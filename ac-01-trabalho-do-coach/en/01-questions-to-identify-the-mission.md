# Questions to identify the mission

Use them in your first conversation with whoever called you in. At the end, say out loud which mission you understood and confirm it with the sponsor.

## Questions
1. What is happening today that made you look for a coach now?
2. Is there already a defined way of working, here or in another area?
3. Has there been a big recent change in structure, roles, or tools?
4. What do you want to be different six months from now?
5. What already works well and must not be lost?

## How to read the answers
| If you hear | Likely mission |
| --- | --- |
| "We know we can improve, but we don't know where" | Diagnosis |
| "We want to work differently" | Transformation |
| "We want what the other area has" | Expansion |
| "We just changed and need support" | Hypercare |
| "It works, and we want it to keep working" | Sustainment |
| "We stopped and want to get going again" | Restart |
