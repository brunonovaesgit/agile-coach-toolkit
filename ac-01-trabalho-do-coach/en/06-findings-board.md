# Diagnosis findings board

Describe the system, never the people. When you report back to leadership, bring three findings and three hypotheses, and end by asking for a decision.

## The six lenses
| Lens | Core question | Where to look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flow | How long does an idea take to reach the customer, and where does it wait? | One real request, end to end |
| Decisions | Who decides what, and how long does it take? | Forums, committees, approval levels |
| Money | How is the work funded? | Planning cycle and initiative approval |
| Metrics | What does leadership track every week? | Dashboards and reports actually used |
| Structure | Are teams organized around the customer or around specialties? | Org chart, dependencies between teams |
| Leadership | Which leadership behaviors speed up the work? | Leadership meetings, one-on-one conversations |
