# Workshop: follow a request

- **Goal:** show the real path of a request, with work time and wait time at each step.
- **When to use:** during diagnosis, and again months later, to compare.
- **Who to involve:** the coach runs it; one person from each area the request went through.
- **Duration:** one to two weeks of short interviews, plus a 90-minute session.
- **Materials:** a wall (physical or Miro/Mural), the request timeline, this script.

## Step by step
1. **Pick the request (30 min).** Recent, finished, typical, not exceptional.
2. **Rebuild the timeline (1 to 2 days).** Tool history, approval emails, minutes. Entry and exit date in each area.
3. **Talk to each area (20 to 30 min each).**
   - When did this request reach you?
   - What did you need to do?
   - How long did it wait here, and why?
   - What would make it easier?
4. **Draw the path.** One line with the steps, separating work and waiting.
5. **Present it to the participants (90 min).** No names, no blame. Ask: what surprises you? Where is the longest wait?
6. **Record the findings** in the findings board.

## Remote version
Video calls; the wall on a shared online board during the presentation.

## Pitfalls
- Picking an exceptional request.
- Turning the presentation into a hunt for culprits.
- Drawing conclusions from a single request: follow two or three before stating a pattern.
